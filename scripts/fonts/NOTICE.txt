Fonts in this folder are ASCII subsets made for embedding in the profile SVGs.
Under the SIL Open Font License, subsets are modified versions, so both GitHub
families are renamed and do not use their Reserved Font Names:

  monasans-*.woff2     subset of Mona Sans (OFL-MonaSans.txt), renamed "Profile Sans"
  monaspace-400.woff2  subset of Monaspace Neon (OFL-Monaspace.txt), renamed "Profile Mono"

The karate motion study (scripts/media/karate-src.svg) carries its own embedded font.
